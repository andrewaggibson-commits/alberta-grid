About

Figures come from the Current Supply Demand report published by the Alberta Electric System Operator (AESO), which updates every few minutes. A scheduled job reads it about every five minutes, so what you see is typically 5–10 minutes old. This site is not affiliated with AESO.

  • Generation is AESO’s total net generation (TNG) for each fuel group.
  • Transfers are flows on the interties with British Columbia, Montana and Saskatchewan; imports into Alberta are shown as positive.
  • Demand is the Alberta Internal Load (AIL): generation plus imports, minus exports.
  • Times are Alberta local time (America/Edmonton), as published by AESO.

The design is adapted from Kate Morley’s open-source grid dashboard (CC0).

Carbon intensity

A rough estimate: each fuel group’s output multiplied by a typical direct (smokestack) emissions factor, divided by total generation. It ignores imports, construction and fuel supply chains, and real plant-by-plant emissions vary.
